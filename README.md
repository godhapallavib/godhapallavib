## 👩‍🔬 About Me

I’m a **Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Illinois Institute of Technology (Chicago)** and a **Graduate Research Assistant** in **Programming Languages & Formal Methods**. My research develops **cost-aware information-flow control (IFC)** for **GPU/CUDA programs**, with **thread-indexed semantics**, **shared-memory bank-conflict cost models**, and **Rocq/Coq mechanization** of soundness and noninterference.

- **Advisors:** Dr. **Farzaneh Derakhshan**, Dr. **Stefan K. Muller**  
- **Interests:** Programming Languages, Formal Verification, Language-based Security, Program Logics  
- **Currently:** Mechanizing the metatheory in **Rocq**.
